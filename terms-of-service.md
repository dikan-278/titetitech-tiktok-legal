Last updated: 2026-11-07

This is a private, single-user analytics tool built and operated by the owner of the TikTok account `@tite_titech` ("the owner"). It is not a public product or service offered to other users.

1. **Who can use this app.** Only the owner's own TikTok account, authorized by the owner through TikTok Login Kit. This app does not accept or process logins from any other TikTok account.
2. **What the app does.** After the owner grants consent via TikTok's OAuth flow, the app calls the TikTok Display API (`video.list`) to read the owner's own public video list and public engagement counters (views, likes, comments, shares). The app does not post, edit, or delete any content on TikTok.
3. **No resale, no third-party access.** The data retrieved is used solely by the owner, in a local, private dashboard, to review the performance of their own content over time. It is not sold, shared, or made available to any third party.
